1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SarkariDisha ("we", "us") handles information in connection with your use of this website. We are a small, independently run information portal, and we aim to keep our data practices as simple and transparent as possible.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

SarkariDisha does not require account registration, does not ask visitors for personal details, and does not collect names, phone numbers, addresses, or payment information through this website. Filling out and submitting an actual job application happens entirely on the official government or recruiting body's own website, not on SarkariDisha.

3. Cookies and Advertising

This website shows advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

We may also use basic, privacy-respecting analytics (such as anonymized visit counts) to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

4. External Links

This website links out to official government websites, application portals, and downloadable notifications for your convenience. Once you leave SarkariDisha via one of these links, that external website's own privacy policy applies — we have no control over, and no access to, any data you may provide there.

5. Admin Access

Listings on this website are added and managed by site administrators through a separate, password-protected admin panel. Admin login credentials are stored and authenticated securely via our backend service provider and are never visible to site visitors.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is intended to provide general information about government recruitment and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in how the website operates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the contact details provided on this website (if any).

SarkariDisha is an independent information portal. Not affiliated with any government body.
